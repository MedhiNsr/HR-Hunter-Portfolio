# Business & Product Decisions

## Fit before volume
The workflow is designed to prevent automation from turning into indiscriminate mass application. Discovery and document generation happen only after fit analysis and eligibility controls.

## Seniority is a decision constraint
Keyword overlap must not hide a material experience gap. Seniority and experience are analysed separately and influence eligibility.

## Separate analysis from generation
Job analysis, fit assessment and document generation are distinct stages so persuasive generated text cannot silently redefine the underlying assessment.

## Preserve a source of truth
Tailoring should improve relevance without inventing credentials, responsibilities or experience. Candidate information remains the grounding source for generated outputs.

## Quality gate after generation
Successful generation does not mean a document is ready to send. Documents are validated before approval and external action.

## Human-in-the-loop
The implementation includes a dedicated human-review path and approval remains part of consequential external actions.

## Stateful workflow
Applications have statuses, contacts, replies and follow-up dates. Persisting this state reduces duplicated actions and supports reporting.

## AI-assisted development
The prototype was developed iteratively with AI coding assistance. My contribution is the problem framing, workflow design, business rules, testing scenarios, product decisions and validation of the resulting system.
